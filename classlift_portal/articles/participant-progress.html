Keep Learning Progress Connected to Every Lesson

Help teachers and students reflect on completed lessons, share feedback, and prepare for what comes next.

A lesson may end when the scheduled time is over, but the learning does not. Students may still have questions, while teachers may want to record what was covered, note areas for improvement, or suggest practice.

When those details live in separate messages or depend on memory, useful context can disappear. ClassLift keeps lesson notes and feedback attached to completed sessions so both sides can refer to the same record.

Keep a Record of What Was Learned

Teachers can add notes to completed one-on-one sessions, including topics covered, learning observations, and suggestions for continued practice.

This creates a practical lesson history: a quick way to revisit what happened without searching through messages or relying on memory.

Give Students a Voice After Class

Students can review completed lessons and add their own notes, questions, or comments. They might ask for more practice with a difficult concept or flag something they would like to revisit.

Those responses give teachers useful insight before the next session and make it easier to respond to the learner's actual needs.

More Than Attendance. A Record of Learning.

Attendance shows which lessons took place. Progress notes explain what happened within them.

By bringing lesson history, teacher observations, and student feedback together, ClassLift gives tutors a clearer view of the learning journey and helps each session connect to the next.

Make the Next Lesson More Meaningful

Every completed lesson leaves useful information behind. ClassLift helps teachers and students find it, respond to it, and use it to move forward.

Remember what was learned. Share what matters. Build on every lesson.
