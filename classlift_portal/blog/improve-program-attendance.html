BEST PRACTICES · 4 MIN READ

A calmer way to improve attendance

Better attendance starts with making it easier for families and staff to know what happens next.

Attendance is more than a number on a report. It is a signal about access, communication, and whether a program fits the lives of the people it serves.

Make the next session visible

Send reminders early, keep schedules current, and give staff one clear place to see changes. Consistency removes avoidable uncertainty.

Follow up with context

A missed session deserves a thoughtful follow-up, not just a red mark. Record the reason, notice patterns, and use that context to offer practical support.

Turn patterns into action

Review attendance regularly with your team. Small, timely adjustments are easier to make than a major intervention after a term has already slipped away.
