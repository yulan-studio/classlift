# ClassLift project instructions

Before changing business behavior, read `Docs/business-rules.md` and the relevant sections of `Docs/ClassLift Database Design and Course Enrollment Workflows.md`.

Treat `Docs/business-rules.md` as the canonical concise record of user-confirmed business rules. When the user states, changes, or clarifies a business rule:

1. Apply the rule to the requested implementation.
2. Update `Docs/business-rules.md` in the same change unless the user asks not to.
3. Replace or mark superseded rules instead of retaining contradictory active rules.
4. Record unresolved ambiguity under **Open questions**; do not present an assumption as a confirmed rule.

Keep the user manual understandable to staff and customers. Do not expose database fields, internal class names, or implementation details unless they are necessary for operating the product.
